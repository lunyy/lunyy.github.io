Jihye Oh

Integrated M.S.–Ph.D. candidate, Korea University

I am a Ph.D. candidate at MAILAB in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Korea University, advised by Prof. Tae-Eui Kam.

My research interests are generative AI, LLM alignment and agentic AI, and medical AI. My first-author work spans generative models of functional brain networks and LLM alignment for text-to-video prompt optimization.

26 publications so far — 6 as first or co-first author, 6 as second author — including WACV, CVPR, MICCAI, ICCV Workshop, ICASSP, ISBI and IEEE JBHI.

Currently: agentic LLM systems for medical reasoning, and extending PT-Diff toward generative augmentation that generalizes across imaging sites.
